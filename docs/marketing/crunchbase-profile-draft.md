## Full description (About section)

> PropertyIQ is an AI-powered real estate market intelligence platform for homebuyers, renters, investors, and real estate professionals. It generates a single PropertyIQ Score (1–99) that predicts a market's three-year performance relative to its state, built from four transparent monthly signals: Zillow home-value momentum (12-month and 3-month) plus Realtor.com median days on market and price-cut share. The equal-weight formula has no fitted parameters and no black box, and it is validated out-of-sample across more than 20 years of data. PropertyIQ scores 900+ US metros, 3,000+ counties, and 29,000+ ZIP codes, with interactive maps, AI-generated market reports, and a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that lets AI agents query its data directly.
