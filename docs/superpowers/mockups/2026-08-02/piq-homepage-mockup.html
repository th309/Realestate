  1. Every emoji is gone. All icons are inline Lucide SVG at stroke-width:2, linecap:round — drawn from the set your codebase already imports: Target, BarChart3, TrendingUp, Map, Search, Sparkles, MapPin, FileText, Building2, Check, ArrowRight, Layers, ShieldCheck.
  2. Defined once as an SVG sprite and referenced with <use>. In the real build these are just lucide-react components, so nothing here needs hand-maintaining.
  3. Icons sit in rounded tinted tiles, matching how PropertyWiz treats theirs and how your pricing page already renders Lucide icons in containers.
  4. Section order mirrors theirs: hero → persona toggle → three steps → platform features → testimonials → final CTA.
  5. One section-header formula everywhere: coloured uppercase kicker with a small icon → big centred H2 → grey subhead → chips.
  6. Two accents, split by role. Indigo owns nav and product chrome; green owns persona, steps, and the closing CTA — their indigo/teal division.
  7. Four real testimonials, verbatim. Two columns rather than their three-up, because your quotes run longer than theirs. Jordan K. leads and carries a "Why they switched" tag — it's the only quote about MCP, which is the capability no competitor has.
  8. Blog is a two-column grid with 16:9 thumbnails. These are generated data cards — headline number over a branded gradient — which your content-pipeline Chromium renderer can produce for all 77 posts.
