# Topic: What Real Estate Agents Can Do with the PropertyIQ MCP

Audience: real estate agents (and broker/team leads).
Angle: the PropertyIQ MCP connects live market intelligence directly into an
AI assistant (like Claude) — the agent asks in plain English, the assistant
calls PropertyIQ's tools and answers with real data. No dashboards, no exports.

Every tool named below is a real, live MCP tool. Do not invent tools or rename
these. All numbers must come from the approved-claims rules in README.md.

## The hook

"Your AI assistant just became a market analyst. Connect PropertyIQ's MCP and
ask it real questions about real markets — it answers with live data, not
guesses."

## Six agent workflows (all real tools)

1. **Find your farm area** — `farm_area_analysis`: top farm areas in a metro,
   ranked with agent-oriented context. Ask: "Best farm areas in the Nashville
   metro right now?"
2. **Walk into listings armed** — `listing_presentation_data`: market data
   assembled for a listing presentation. Ask: "Build my listing presentation
   data for ZIP 37206."
3. **Prep a buyer consult in minutes** — `buyer_consultation_brief`: a
   buyer-ready brief on any market. Pair with `neighborhood_comparison` when
   the buyer is torn between two areas.
4. **Win relocation clients** — `relocation_package`: market context for a
   family moving metro-to-metro. Pair with `referral_network_finder` to find
   the referral on the other end.
5. **Stay in front of your sphere** — `monthly_market_update_email`: a
   data-grounded monthly update narrative for your contacts;
   `generate_cold_email` and `generate_linkedin_post` draft outreach grounded
   in the same live data.
6. **For team leads/brokers** — `agent_recruitment_pitch` and
   `brokerage_market_coverage_report`: recruiting and coverage stories backed
   by market data instead of vibes.

## Why it's credible (approved claims only)

- Coverage: 900+ metros, 3,000+ counties, 29,000+ ZIPs scored monthly.
- The PropertyIQ Score is a 1-99 momentum signal — 50 = state average; higher
  scores have historically outperformed relative to their state (top band
  81-99: +0.38pp annualized 3-year excess vs state; bottom band 1-20:
  -1.29pp). Momentum words only; A/B/C/F letters are data-confidence, never a
  score grade.
- Data sources: Zillow, Realtor.com, Census, FRED, BLS.
