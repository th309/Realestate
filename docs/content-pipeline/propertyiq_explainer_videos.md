# PropertyIQ Explainer Videos — Production Shot Lists

**Tone:** Apple keynote. Confident. Declarative. Negative space.
**Pacing:** ~120 wpm. Honor every pause. Silence is the punch.
**Brand colors:** #3949AB primary · #1A237E dark · #00C853 accent · #1A1A2E surface
**Music:** Minimal underscore. Drop 6-8 dB under VO. Cut entirely under text-reveal beats.
**Format:** 1080p / 4K, 30fps. Record voice and screen separately, sync in Descript or Premiere.

## Cross-Cutting Production Notes

### Visual language (consistent across all 5)

- Dark surface (#1A1A2E) for full-screen text moments. Always white type.
- Accent green (#00C853) sparingly. Reserve for scores, deltas, "good" indicators.
- Primary blue (#3949AB) for UI chrome, progression bars, active states.
- 0.3s fade-to-black between major beats. Never hard cut a beat change.

### Apple-style text-reveal moments

These are the "Steve walks across the stage" beats. Don't skip them. Used above:

- "There has to be a better way."
- "Until now."
- "That's not a strategy."
- "There's a better way to prep."

White, large, centered, on dark surface. 1.5s in, 1.5s hold, 0.5s out.

### Music direction

Search "Apple keynote" or "minimal cinematic tech" on Artlist, Musicbed, or Epidemic Sound. Look for:

- Sparse piano + subtle synth pad
- Builds slowly, no sudden drops
- Resolves clean for the closing logo

Cut music entirely under text-reveal beats. The silence is the point.

### Voiceover delivery

- Slower than feels natural. Target 110-120 wpm.
- Drop pitch slightly at the end of declarative sentences. Don't uptalk.
- Pauses marked [pause] in the script are not optional. Count one Mississippi minimum.
- Record tight to the mic. 6 inches. Pop filter. Treated room or thick blanket fort.

### Tools

- **Screen capture:** Descript or ScreenFlow (both let you re-record VO and snap to scenes).
- **Editing:** Descript is fastest if you're not a video editor. Premiere or DaVinci if you are.
- **Captions:** Descript auto-captions are solid. Burn in for social cuts.
- **VO recording:** Even an iPhone Voice Memos in a closet beats a bad USB mic in an echoey room.

### Social cuts (9:16 vertical, 15-30s)

Each long video should be carved into a vertical short for YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok. Lift these beats:

| Source           | Best lift                  | Cut       |
| ---------------- | -------------------------- | --------- |
| Hero             | The score reveal           | 0:14-0:32 |
| MCP              | The before/after demo      | 0:20-0:50 |
| Agent            | Listing prep + buyer brief | 0:18-0:45 |
| Investor         | Deal analyzer              | 0:32-0:55 |
| Site walkthrough | Compare markets            | 0:55-1:18 |

### Recording order recommendation

1. **MCP demo first.** It's the highest-leverage video and the most technically involved (Claude responses need pre-recording). Get it right while you're fresh.
2. **Hero second.** Short, sets the brand voice for everything else.
3. **Site walkthrough third.** Pure screen capture, easy day.
4. **Agent and Investor last.** These reuse a lot of footage from the site walkthrough so editing them last lets you grab clips you already shot.

### One more thing

Every video closes with the logo holding for 3 seconds in silence. That hold is the most Apple thing in the entire production. Don't shorten it.
