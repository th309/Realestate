# Content-Pipeline Visual Style Taxonomy

**Purpose:** The named classification system for visual styles in the Style Library
(`/admin/content-pipeline/style-references`). When identifying the style of a URL
(channel, post, video, image), classify against THESE names — do not invent new
descriptions ad hoc. The same URL should get the same answer next month. If something
genuinely doesn't fit, propose a new named entry and add it here.

**Researched:** 2026-07-27, cross-platform (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok), RE/finance niche.
Refresh cadence: revisit trend statuses roughly every 2 quarters.

**Identify-a-URL workflow:** fetch/screenshot the target → match against the 10 named
styles below (markers: palette, typography, layout, video production) → report closest
style + confidence + specific markers seen → on approval, add to the Style Library with
the taxonomy name in the label.

## The 10 Named Styles

### 1. Market-Data Authority Card

- **Look:** One stat or one chart per card. Huge headline number (bold or mono sans),
  small context line, cited source, flat clean design, brand-locked 2–3 colors.
- **Video:** Animated chart builds, number counters, calm narration.
- **Examples:** @narresearch, ResiClub / Lance Lambert charts, Coffee & Contracts
  "Market Snapshot" templates.
- **Status (2026):** Rising — named a top reach format for 2026 carousels.
- **PIQ fit:** Perfect. Natural lane for PIQ scores and market stats; fully automatable.

### 2. Doom-Data Alarm

- **Look:** Black/white/red palette. Condensed ALL-CAPS banner ("NEWS ALERT"), red
  crashing chart or US heat map, stat chip with giant white number ("-71%"), worried
  face optional, yellow caps with thick black stroke. Brand logos as props (Zillow
  sign, Fed building).
- **Video:** Green-screen or PiP over charts/listings, red circle + arrow annotations,
  cursor zoom-ins, urgent verbal pacing, cuts every 3–15s.
- **Examples:** Graham Stephan, Meet Kevin, Reventure Consulting (direct PIQ comp —
  their channel funnels to their data app posting ~2×/day in this template).
- **Status:** Dominant for macro/housing news. Exaggerated shock faces softening
  toward "authentic concern."
- **PIQ fit:** Strong — map + stat chip + alarm type works faceless.

### 3. Bright Data Explainer

- **Look:** White/light studio background, black type + one green accent word, 2–4
  words in heavy geometric sans, friendly face pointing at a physical/3D data prop
  (coin-stack bar chart), red curved dotted arrow.
- **Video:** Talking head + frequent full-screen motion-graphic inserts (animated
  tables, counters), medium-fast jump cuts, upbeat neutral music, visual proof inside
  first 15 seconds. The winning long-form finance format for 2026.
- **Examples:** Humphrey Yang, Erika Kullberg long-form.
- **Status:** Rising/dominant. Deep-dive explainers with data viz = 65% of top
  finance libraries.
- **PIQ fit:** Strong — the motion-graphic inserts are the replicable part.

### 4. Bold-Type Hook

- **Look:** 3–5 word extra-bold headline (Anton / Bebas Neue / Montserrat), white or
  yellow with heavy black stroke, one consistent template across the grid. Color-coded
  by content type: green = educational, red/pink = urgency, blue = trust,
  purple = luxury. Carousel variant: slide 1 is a pure typography hook; elements bleed
  off the right edge to force the swipe. Rising sub-variant: text-behind-subject.
- **Video:** Freeze-frame face + text cover; fast hook-first pacing.
- **Examples:** Glennda Baker, Tori Dunlap (@herfirst100k), @personalfinanceclub.
- **Status:** Dominant on TikTok/IG; reinforced by 2026 big-typography trend and
  TikTok-as-search (covers as "tiny landing pages," keyword-readable).
- **PIQ fit:** Perfect — directly replicable in Remotion.

### 5. Cinematic Luxury Editorial

- **Look:** Twilight/golden-hour drone or wide hero shot, teal-and-orange grade, warm
  interior lights. ONE text element: full price in giant thin elegant white numerals
  ("$53,000,000" — never abbreviated). Tiny human for scale. On IG grids: muted
  neutrals (cream/taupe/black) + one signature brand color, high-contrast serifs,
  magazine layouts.
- **Video:** 4K gimbal walkthrough, drone establishing shots, long takes, wall-reveal
  transitions, ambient score, rooms allowed to "breathe."
- **Examples:** Enes Yilmazer, Erik Conover, Dawn McKenna Group, Tatiana Londono,
  Ryan Serhant listing films.
- **Status:** Dominant in luxury RE, holding — but the sterile ultra-polished end is
  losing algorithmic ground to raw content.
- **PIQ fit:** Good — hero still + price overlay replicable for listing/market content.

### 6. Faceless Voiceover Essay

- **Look:** Cinematic b-roll still, no face, minimal text, moody dark-mode-friendly
  grade.
- **Video:** Slow cinematic b-roll + voiceover essay, minimal on-screen text.
- **Examples:** Quiet Quest and similar faceless finance channels.
- **Status:** The fastest-scaling emerging finance lane for 2026; lowest production
  overhead.
- **PIQ fit:** The most automatable video format — no persona required at all.

### 7. Soft-Gradient Premium App (Glassmorphism)

- **Look:** Soft sunset gradients (purple → deep blue → peach), frosted-glass card
  overlays, rounded corners, generous whitespace, clean sans. Reads like a fintech
  app, not a poster.
- **Status:** Rising — explicitly named the 2026 replacement for harsh neon blocks.
- **PIQ fit:** Strong brand match — rhymes with the M3/indigo PropertyIQ design system.

### 8. Green-Screen Receipt Commentary

- **Look:** Creator keyed over the evidence — Zillow/Realtor listing screenshot, FRED
  chart, news headline, tweet. Native platform text (white classic font / TikTok Sans,
  caption bubble) holds the hook. Deliberately un-designed; emoji-heavy.
- **Video:** 15–45s one-concept rants; face bottom-corner, evidence fills frame.
- **Examples:** Vivian Tu (@yourrichbff), Humphrey Yang, agents debunking market
  myths over listing screenshots.
- **Status:** Dominant on TikTok, stable; still being iterated ("fake green screen").
- **PIQ fit:** Weak for now — needs a human persona; reference only unless an
  avatar/presenter is added.

### 9. Lo-Fi Authentic / Scrapbook

- **Look:** Phone-shot, ungraded, natural light, native text overlays. IG variant:
  photo dumps (8–20 loose images), torn-paper/Polaroid/sticker-cutout collage,
  handwritten fonts, Notes-app screenshots. YouTube variant: deadpan anti-thumbnail
  (raw video frame, zero text — ClearValue Tax; ironic deadpan — The Plain Bagel).
- **Video:** Handheld POV/day-in-the-life, trending audio, serialized progress
  counters ("Day 30 of paying off debt"); or calm long-take talking head.
- **Status:** Rising fastest platform-wide. Backed by Canva's "Imperfect by Design"
  2026 trend and Instagram's stated prioritization of raw content over polish.
- **PIQ fit:** Tricky — automated content faking "authentic" is a brand risk. Know it,
  don't chase it.

### 10. Screenshot Meme

- **Look:** Tweet/X screenshot on plain (black/white) background, reaction-image memes
  (The Office is an RE staple), impact-caption photos, hyper-local market memes.
  System UI fonts — the anti-brand look is the brand. Litquidity differentiates on
  clean sharp formatting vs fuzzy low-res meme accounts.
- **Examples:** The Broke Agent / BAM, Litquidity, @re_memes.
- **Status:** Dominant in the agent-to-agent RE niche; mature/stable in finance.
- **PIQ fit:** Situational — high engagement, off-brand for "The Smart Friend" unless
  done carefully.

## Avoid

- **AI-composite hyperreal chaos** (exploding-Tesla hyperreal posters): rising but
  polarizing; collides with the authenticity trend and trust-first positioning.
- **The undifferentiated middle:** semi-polished generic graphics with gradients and
  icons — matches nothing currently trending, and is exactly what un-steered AI
  generation produces. The whole point of the Style Library is to avoid this.

## Cross-Cutting Rules (2025–2026)

- Thumbnails: 0–4 words, one dominant subject, 2–3 colors, legible at 120px.
- Faces: 72% of high-traffic thumbnails have one; sad/worried outperforms joyful
  (2.3M vs 1.5M avg views).
- Dark-mode optimization rising: dark bg + neon accent (cyan/purple/green).
- Instagram Jan 2025 grid change: profile previews are 3:4; reel covers are
  1080×1350 with all faces/text inside the central 1080×1080 square.
- TikTok cover safe zones: avoid top 130px, bottom 320px, right 120px.
- The niche split is rigid — tour, advice, and data channels each keep a distinct
  uniform; top creators do not mix them. Pick style per content purpose (see
  `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-27-content-purpose-taxonomy-design.md`), don't blend.
