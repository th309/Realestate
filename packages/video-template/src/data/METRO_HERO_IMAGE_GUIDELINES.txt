Metro hero images (long-form CBSA lookups)
============================================

Goal
----
Use views that viewers who know the metro will recognize immediately—the
“postcard” angle locals and tourists associate with that city—not a generic
stock aerial or obscure angle.

Selection checklist
-------------------
1. Landmark-forward — Iconic vantage (waterfront, famous park, named viewpoint)
   where signature towers, bridges, or geography read clearly at a glance.
2. Rights — Prefer Wikimedia Commons (or your own licensed asset). Note author
   and license; include in video credits when the license requires it.
3. Timelessness — Clear daylight or classic skyline shots; avoid dated promo
   imagery unless that is the intent.
4. Technical — Wide panoramas work well full-bleed in 16:9; keep file sizes
   reasonable (Commons thumbnails are fine).

Adding a new metro
------------------
1. Choose one definitive shot per CBSA (same city people mean when they say
   the market name).
2. Add the CBSA code and HTTPS image URL to:
   - packages/video-template/src/data/metro-hero-curated-urls.json
   - packages/backend/src/content-pipeline/data/metro-hero-source-urls.json
   (keep both files in sync so Studio and production renders match.)
