[PropertyIQ scores St. Louis a 91 out of 100](/markets/st-louis-mo) as of February 28, 2026.

The median listing price is $278,175 as of February 2026. The market is 4.4% below fundamental value. A median-income household in St. Louis can qualify to buy at median price -- a combination that is uncommon in 2026.

## Affordability Is the Core Story

The income needed to buy at current prices is approximately $73,938. The median household income in St. Louis is $78,225 (2023 Census). The gap is roughly $4,300. That alignment between income and entry price is what makes St. Louis structurally different from coastal markets where that gap runs $50,000 or more.

## Demand Is Executing at Full Asking Price

Pending-to-active ratio: 0.6763 as of February 2026. For every 100 homes listed, 68 are going under contract. Homes average 55 days on market. The sale-to-list ratio is 100% as of November 2025. Sellers are receiving exactly what they ask for, on average. Price cuts affect only 12.36% of listings.

## Prices Are Stable With Positive Momentum

Home values are up 0.5% year over year as of February 2026. Zillow forecasts 2.2% near-term appreciation as of December 2025. Home sales are rising: up 2.91% year over year. Stable prices and rising sales volume point in the same direction.

## Inventory Is Growing but Absorbed

There are 4,950 homes for sale, up 10.78% year over year. The 68% pending ratio shows demand is absorbing the supply growth. New inventory is not stacking up; it is moving.

## The Economic Base

Unemployment: 3.5% as of December 2025 -- at or near the national average. Average rent: approximately $1,387/month as of December 2025. Zillow median home value: approximately $264,796 as of January 2026. Monthly gross rent-to-price ratio: roughly 0.52%.

St. Louis sits alongside [Detroit](/markets/detroit-mi) and [Cleveland](/markets/cleveland-elyria-oh) as a Midwest market where the fundamentals are intact and the entry price still makes sense for a broad population of buyers.
