A 1031 exchange is one of the highest-stakes real estate transactions an investor will execute. The replacement property must be identified within 45 days. The exchange must close within 180 days. The investor is moving capital that carries embedded gains accumulated over years or decades, and any mistake in market selection does not just affect future returns. It affects the compounding of capital that has already been working.

The best markets for a 1031 exchange in 2026 are not necessarily the most exciting markets. They are the markets where the underlying fundamentals are strongest, where values are likely to hold and grow over a 10-to-20-year horizon, and where the income thesis is supported by genuine tenant demand. The 45-day identification window creates urgency that can push investors toward the wrong choices. This analysis exists to give 1031 investors a data-supported shortlist before that clock starts.

PropertyIQ scores every U.S. metro on a 0-100 index updated monthly using Zillow, Realtor.com, Census, and economic data. All scores referenced in this post are effective February 28, 2026. A PropertyIQ Score above 85 represents a market where supply is structurally limited and demand is documented and sustained.

## What a 1031 Exchange Investor Actually Needs From a Market

The 1031 exchange investor has a profile that differs from a typical acquisition. They have capital from a sale that has already been invested and appreciated. They need a replacement property that is equal or greater in value to the relinquished property. And they are making this decision under time pressure that most acquisitions do not carry.

Four criteria separate the right 1031 markets from the wrong ones:

**Criterion 1: Durable fundamentals, not speculative upside.** A 1031 investor is not looking for the next emerging market. They are looking for a market where the capital they reinvest is as protected as possible over a long hold period. High PropertyIQ Scores achieved through structural supply constraints and income-supported demand represent durable fundamentals. High scores achieved through speculative momentum represent temporary conditions that can reverse.

**Criterion 2: Income-generating from day one.** A 1031 exchange requires the replacement property to produce income to justify the deferred gain. Markets where rental demand is strong and vacancy is low support the income thesis that makes the exchange work as a capital preservation and compounding strategy.

**Criterion 3: Not overheated.** A market priced significantly above fundamental value is a risk for a 1031 investor who is entering at current prices. Markets that score high because of genuine supply-demand imbalance rather than speculative pricing are safer 1031 destinations. When fundamental value analysis shows a market is at or below fair value while the PropertyIQ Score is strong, the risk of price correction is lower than in overheated markets.

**Criterion 4: Legal and regulatory stability.** A 1031 investor with a long hold horizon needs to be able to operate in the replacement market for the life of their intended hold. States with stable, predictable landlord-tenant law reduce the regulatory risk that a 10-to-20-year hold carries.

The markets below score between 88 and 99 on the PropertyIQ index, are not materially overpriced relative to fundamental value, and pass the income thesis test.

## Best Markets for a 1031 Exchange in 2026

### Rochester, NY: Score 99/100 | 21 Months at Maximum Score | The Most Documented Market in the Dataset

Rochester, New York is the most consistently documented high-performing market in the PropertyIQ dataset as of February 2026. The market has scored 99 out of 100 for 21 consecutive months. No other major metro in the current dataset has maintained that score for longer.

For a 1031 exchange investor, consistency matters more than a single high score. A market that reached 99 last month may be a temporary spike. A market that has held 99 for nearly two years has demonstrated structural supply-demand imbalance that does not reverse quickly. The underlying conditions are embedded in the market, not borrowed from a favorable moment.

The Rochester score reflects specific measurable signals. There are more homes under contract than available for sale, a pending-to-active ratio exceeding 1.0 that has been sustained for an extended period. Buyers pay above asking price. The affordability gap, the difference between the income required to buy at median price and the actual local median household income, is the smallest of any market in this analysis. Rochester is the closest to genuinely income-accessible of any high-scoring market in the Northeast.

The Rochester economy is anchored by higher education and healthcare: the University of Rochester, Rochester Institute of Technology, and a cluster of major medical institutions provide a stable, recession-resistant employment base. These institutions have existed for decades and are not going anywhere. They generate consistent, income-qualified tenant demand from graduate students, medical professionals, and healthcare workers across multiple income tiers.

New York state landlord-tenant law is not the most favorable in the country. That is the honest caveat for a 1031 investor considering Rochester. The legal framework is more tenant-protective than Midwest alternatives. A 1031 investor with a long hold horizon needs to factor that into operational planning and ensure they are working with an experienced local property manager who knows the regulatory environment.

The offsetting factor: in a market scoring 99 for 21 straight months, vacancy cycles are short. The legal environment matters most when tenants are hard to find and turnover is costly. In Rochester, replacement tenants are readily available when units turn. The practical impact of tenant-protective law is lower in a high-demand market than in a balanced or soft one.

### Worcester, MA: Score 95/100 | 12 Months at 94+ | University Anchor Demand

Worcester, Massachusetts scores 95 out of 100 on the PropertyIQ Score as of February 28, 2026. The market has held at 94 or above for 12 consecutive months, establishing it as one of the most sustained high-scoring markets in New England.

For 1031 investors, Worcester's 12-month consistency at 94+ is a critical signal. A market that scores 95 in a single month might be reacting to a seasonal inventory tightening. A market that holds at 94+ for a full year is documenting a structural imbalance between supply and demand that has persisted through seasonal variation, rate cycles, and national market shifts.

Worcester's demand score is 92 out of 100. Homes sell at full asking price in under 50 days. Sales volume is up 19.2% year over year, a figure that confirms demand is not hypothetical: buyers and renters are absorbing available inventory at an accelerating rate.

The tenant demand foundation in Worcester is built on multiple pillars. Clark University, College of the Holy Cross, Worcester Polytechnic Institute, and UMass Medical School collectively generate a student, graduate student, and medical professional population that creates continuous rental absorption. UMass Memorial Health, one of the largest employers in Central Massachusetts, employs thousands of healthcare workers who rent across multiple price tiers.

Median household income in Worcester is $93,561, one of the higher figures in New England outside Boston. That income level supports rental rates that justify acquisition at current entry prices. A 1031 investor reinvesting proceeds from a sold asset into Worcester can underwrite rental income supported by an educated, high-income tenant pool rather than a population renting because ownership is inaccessible to them.

Massachusetts landlord-tenant law is more tenant-protective than Midwest alternatives, similar in complexity to New York. A 1031 investor in Worcester needs to factor regulatory compliance into their operational model and work with experienced local management. The market conditions support the investment case despite the legal complexity, but ignoring the legal environment would be a planning error.

### Grand Rapids, MI: Score 93/100 | 2-Year Consistency | Midwest Legal Certainty

Grand Rapids, Michigan scores 93 out of 100 on the PropertyIQ index as of February 2026 and has held above 89 for every month of the past two years. For a 1031 investor prioritizing legal predictability alongside market strength, Michigan offers one of the cleaner operating environments in the dataset.

Michigan's landlord-tenant law is investor-favorable by national standards. Eviction timelines are efficient. Security deposit rules are clear and well-defined. The legal framework for landlord-tenant relationships has been relatively stable and predictable, which is exactly what a 1031 investor with a 10-to-20-year hold horizon needs.

The Grand Rapids market signal is built on an 86% listing absorption rate. For every 100 active listings, 86 are under contract. Inventory is declining year over year. That structural tightness has been documented consistently for two years, not manufactured by a single quarter of low supply.

Grand Rapids' employment base is diversified across healthcare, advanced manufacturing, food processing, and growing technology and finance sectors. Amazon operates major facilities in the metro. Pfizer has significant operations in the region. Spectrum Health and Mercy Health are among the largest employers. That diversification is what a 1031 investor needs to see: no single employer or sector represents more than a manageable share of total employment.

Population growth in Grand Rapids is positive and among the strongest of any Midwest metro. New residents require housing. The supply side has not kept pace with demand growth, which is the core of the market tightness the 93 score reflects. For a 1031 investor making a long-hold commitment, population growth provides the demographic underpinning that the supply-demand analysis reflects in the score.

Entry prices in Grand Rapids are moderate for a market at 93. A 1031 investor trading up from a smaller property can access this market without the entry barriers of coastal alternatives. The combination of score consistency, legal certainty, employment diversity, and accessible entry creates a profile that serves long-horizon reinvestment well.

For a 1031 investor reinvesting proceeds at scale, undervaluation while the PropertyIQ Score is high means capital deployed in Akron goes further than in fairly-valued or overvalued alternatives. A 1031 investor who identifies a $500,000 replacement property in Akron is acquiring into a market where that price is below fundamental value. Akron's demand score is 92 out of 100. The pending ratio is 0.84. Sale-to-list ratio is 100%. Five-year appreciation is 46.25%, the strongest of any Ohio market in the dataset. New construction sales in a recent month totaled 7 units, confirming that new supply is not pressuring the market.

The Akron employment base includes the University of Akron, Summa Health, Cleveland Clinic affiliates, and a diversified manufacturing sector that has expanded beyond the historical rubber industry. The employment diversity is genuine, not dependent on a single anchor.

Ohio's landlord-tenant law is among the most investor-favorable frameworks in the country. Eviction timelines are efficient. Security deposit rules are clear. The process for recovering a rental unit from a non-paying tenant operates on timelines that small operators and institutional investors can both plan around. For a 1031 investor who will hold for a decade or more, that legal certainty compounds.

St. Louis generates the geographic diversity that a 1031 investor reinvesting a significant amount of capital will eventually need. The Washington University health system, BJC HealthCare, Edward Jones, Centene Corporation, Boeing's defense operations, and a significant financial services cluster provide employment diversity that protects St. Louis from single-sector downturns. ## How to Use PropertyIQ Score in a 1031 Exchange Decision

The 45-day identification window is where most 1031 exchange mistakes happen. The urgency is real and the consequences of failing to identify a replacement property are severe. That urgency creates pressure to accept the first adequate property rather than waiting for a genuinely good one.

PropertyIQ Score is the tool that lets investors pre-build their shortlist before the clock starts. Investors who have already identified the markets they are interested in, scored them, and done basic property-level research in those markets are in a fundamentally different position when their sale closes than investors who are starting the market research process on day one of the 45-day window.

The practical approach:

**Before your relinquished property closes:** Identify two to three markets that score above 85 on the PropertyIQ index, are not materially overvalued relative to fundamental value, and have landlord-tenant environments you can operate in. The markets in this post are a verified starting point.

**Within 48 hours of your close:** Activate your qualified intermediary. Contact property managers in your target markets to identify available inventory before the public listing process. Networked inventory access is the difference between having 10 replacement property options and 3.

**By day 30:** Your replacement properties should be identified and due diligence underway. A 45-day window managed passively becomes a 30-day effective window when you factor in closing coordination.

**On market selection:** PropertyIQ Score predicts how a market is likely to perform over roughly the next three years relative to its state. For a 1031 investor with a 10-to-20-year hold horizon, that forward read matters as a starting screen. The deeper question is whether the structural factors behind the score, supply constraint, income-supported demand, employment diversity, population stability or growth, are durable. The markets in this analysis have demonstrated consistency over 12-to-24 month periods, which is the minimum evidence of structural rather than temporary strength.

The best markets for a 1031 exchange in 2026 are not the markets on the evening news. Rochester at 99 has been the strongest market in the dataset for nearly two years.
