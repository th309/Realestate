  Key Findings

  1. All scores add genuine value — not just riding the market wave
    - Top quintiles have positive excess returns
    - Bottom quintiles have negative excess returns
    - Returns increase monotonically with quintile
  2. InvestorEdge is the strongest predictor
    - 5.49% spread between top and bottom
    - Best at identifying losers (only 10% of bottom quintile beat market)
  3. HomeReady has highest hit rate
    - 89% of top quintile beat the market
    - Best for confident "buy" recommendations
  4. Avoiding losers may be more valuable than picking winners
    - Bottom quintile severely underperforms (10-19% beat rate)
    - Strong "avoid" signal

  Dollar Impact

  On a $500K property over 3 years:
  - Top quintile: +$28K vs median
  - Bottom quintile: -$35K vs median
  - Total value at risk: ~$63K

  Verdict: The scores are statistically validated predictors of excess returns (p < 0.001).
