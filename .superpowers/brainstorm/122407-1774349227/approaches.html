State Label Positioning: 3 Approaches

All are fully dynamic — computed from geometry, no hardcoded positions

A

Better Centroid Algorithm (polylabel)

What changes: Replace bounding-box center with Mapbox's polylabel algorithm — finds the "pole of inaccessibility" (point farthest from any edge). This is how Google Maps and most professional mapping tools place labels.

Fixes: Florida label moves inland. Michigan lands on lower peninsula. Louisiana stays on land.

Doesn't fix: Small NE states still collide — Maryland, Delaware, Connecticut, Rhode Island labels still get hidden by Mapbox collision detection.

Effort: Low — swap ~30 lines in calculateCentroid()
Dependency: @mapbox/polylabel (tiny, by the Mapbox team)
B

Polylabel + Smart Abbreviations for Small States

What changes: Uses polylabel (Approach A) PLUS automatically detects small states by polygon area and switches to 2-letter abbreviation (e.g., "MD $396K" instead of "Maryland $396,102"). Reduces label footprint so Mapbox collision detection keeps them visible.

Fixes: Everything in A, plus small NE states become visible because shorter labels fit without colliding.

Trade-off: Inconsistent label style — some states show full names, others show abbreviations. But this matches how professional maps handle the NE corridor.

Effort: Low-Medium — polylabel + area calculation + conditional text field
Dependency: @mapbox/polylabel + Turf.js area()
C

Polylabel + Zoom-Adaptive Labels

What changes: Uses polylabel (Approach A) PLUS makes labels zoom-aware. At low zoom (full US view): small states show abbreviations + compact values ("MD $396K"). At higher zoom (zoomed into NE): all states show full names + full values ("Maryland $396,102"). Mapbox expressions handle transitions automatically.

Fixes: Everything in A and B, plus labels get richer as you zoom in — best of both worlds.

Trade-off: More complex Mapbox expressions. Label "pops" when crossing zoom thresholds (mitigated with text-size transitions).

Effort: Medium — polylabel + area calc + zoom-dependent Mapbox expressions
Dependency: @mapbox/polylabel + Turf.js area()

My Recommendation: C

Approach C gives you the most polished result. The zoom-adaptive behavior is how professional map tools (Google Maps, Apple Maps) handle label density — show less at overview, more on zoom. The extra complexity is in Mapbox expressions, not app logic, so it's maintainable. A is too incomplete (ignores small states), B is good but C is strictly better for similar effort.
