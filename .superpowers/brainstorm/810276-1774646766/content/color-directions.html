Brand Color Direction

Pick the palette that feels most like PropertyIQ. Consider: trust, data authority, and approachability.

A. Royal Purple (Current)

Primary: #6750A4   Accent: Green

Distinctive in real estate. Conveys innovation and intelligence. Pairs with green for positive metrics. Risk: might feel "tech" more than "real estate."

B. Deep Teal

Primary: #006A6A   Accent: Amber

Trust + sophistication. Common in fintech (Robinhood, Wealthsimple). Feels premium and data-forward. Amber accent for alerts and highlights.

C. Indigo

Primary: #3949AB   Accent: Green

Authority and depth. Feels like a serious analytics platform. Strong association with data/intelligence. Green accent for growth metrics.

D. Slate + Electric Purple

Primary: #1E293B   Accent: #8B5CF6

Dark, premium, modern. Slate base with purple as accent instead of primary. Feels like Stripe/Linear. Very "power user" but still keeps purple DNA.
